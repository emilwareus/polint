`VecDeque` worklist engine, a unified `SolverBudget`/`BudgetStatus` budget model, and a

this plan lifts and generalizes it.

Output: `crates/polint/src/analysis/solver/{mod,budget,policy,engine}.rs`, registered in
`analysis/mod.rs`. Provenance, facts/store, provider wiring, and fixtures come in Plans 02
and 03.
