<objective>
Establish the identity substrate that every subsequent v1.3 phase reads from. Add a new pub(crate) module `analysis::identity` carrying `IdentityRecord` (function + callsite identity with stable signature digest), a provider that projects existing v1.2 `analysis::calls` facts into identity records, a semantic-dedup layer with multiplicity counter, a cache-key contract, a typed store with dangling-reference validation, a diagnostics validator, and a snapshot fixture proving dedup is order-independent. Register `polint.identity` in the kernel provider manifest between `polint.calls` and `polint.abstract_domains`.

Purpose: Identity is the foundation IDENT-02 (renderers, Plan 02), IDENT-03 (categorization, Plan 03), and every later v1.3 phase (semantic graph nodes, solver inputs, dedup keys for scoring) layer on top of. Renderers and categorization in later plans are pure functions of `&IdentityRecord`; without it they cannot exist.

Output: A new `crates/polint/src/analysis/identity/` subtree (mod.rs + 6 files), one provider manifest entry, one fixture pair (`tests/eval-fixtures/identity/dedup/`).
</objective>
