<objective>
Extend the `polint-go-frontend` Go sidecar and the `go::semantic` lowering layer to emit the three RTA input signals GO-05 requires but Phase 46 deliberately deferred (per Phase 46 D-18): **address-taken functions**, **instantiated runtime types** (the "rapid type" set), and **dynamic-callsite dispatch detail**. These are the load-bearing inputs without which an RTA driver is just CHA and cannot lift Go recall toward the 70–90% ceiling (CONTEXT D-05 — "the single most load-bearing decision").

Purpose: Plan 2's `go_rta` fixpoint consumes exactly these facts (instantiated-type filter, method-set matching, address-taken candidate set). The high-cost SSA build is already done in the sidecar (`ssautil.AllPackages` + `prog.Build()`), so this is an additive instruction walk, not a new pipeline.

Output: New crate-private `GoSemantic*` facts (`GoSemanticAddressTakenFact`, `GoSemanticInstantiatedTypeFact`, `GoSemanticDynamicDispatchFact`) emitted by the sidecar, lowered, stored, validated, cache-keyed, and exposed via `AnalysisDb` accessors. No semantic-graph constraint emission and no solver work in this plan — that is Plan 2.
</objective>
