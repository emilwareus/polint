<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Risk | Mitigation |
|----------|------|------------|
| Solver output to refined calls | Solver-derived uncertainty could be laundered into resolved call facts. | Map status/precision conservatively and preserve provenance/input keys. |
| Old heuristic producers | Duplicate dynamic edges could inflate recall. | Remove/demote old producers and assert no duplicate semantic edge stable keys. |
| Cache identity | Solver-only changes could leave stale refined calls. | Fold solver output digest directly into refined-call provider identity. |

## STRIDE Notes

- Tampering: duplicate/stale projections are rejected through validation and cache digest tests.
- Repudiation: projected edges keep solver provenance and contributing fact keys.
- Information disclosure: projection internals stay `pub(crate)` and do not enter public SDK docs.
</threat_model>
