# W4.3 LAND — taint IFDS with sanitizer kills

## Result

- `analysis/ifds::find_taint_paths` is the sole production source-to-sink path
  engine. Its exploded domain carries `IfdsFact::Tainted` over the matched ICFG
  and retains the traversed data-flow edge IDs for deterministic replay.
- `IfdsTaintSolver::kills_taint` applies the sanitizer kill transfer. Present
  sanitizer/barrier edges, sanitizer/barrier models or nodes, and
  query-matched sanitizer call sites terminate the taint fact before it can
  reach a sink.
- Local MIR `CallArgumentToReturn` edges now carry their typed `call_site`
  field. Sanitizer transfer no longer recovers identity from evidence strings.
- `policy_queries::forbidden_data_flows` resolves the existing
  `BarrierPattern::call_any` vocabulary to call-site IDs and supplies those
  kills directly to IFDS. Existing source and sink kinds are unchanged.

## Deleted filters

- Deleted `policy_queries::barrier_covers_path`, which filtered already-found
  reachability paths after solving.
- Deleted `policy_queries::edge_call_site`, including its heuristic
  `call_site=` evidence-string parser.
- Renamed and replaced the reachability-only `find_paths` /
  `IfdsReachabilitySolver` entrypoint. There is no separate reachability path
  engine for forbidden-data-flow queries.

## Regression

- `solver_kills_only_paths_crossing_a_configured_sanitizer` proves sanitizer
  kills are path-specific and that the returned matched path is replayable.
- `solver_reports_not_found_when_sanitizer_kills_every_tainted_path` proves a
  killed fact cannot reach the sink.
- `call_argument_to_return_edge_carries_typed_call_site` proves the production
  local data-flow provider supplies sanitizer identity structurally.
- `data_flow_forbidden_respects_barrier_call` proves the product query passes
  configured sanitizer sites into IFDS.
