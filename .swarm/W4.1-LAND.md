# W4.1 LAND — points-to is the sole TS/JS indirect-call resolver

## Result

- Production registers exactly one TS/JS indirect-call policy: `ts_points_to`.
- The policy projects semantic-graph copy, allocation, field-load, field-store,
  and call constraints into the existing field-sensitive
  `analysis::points_to::solver::solve_points_to` Andersen fixpoint.

  consume those edges through the existing semantic-graph projection.
