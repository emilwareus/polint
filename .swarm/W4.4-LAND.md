# W4.4 LAND — precision-theater cleanup

## Result

- Reachability now stores roots only. The production provider no longer computes
  or persists a direct-call BFS marking that no production consumer used.
- Evaluation scores the engine's complete call-edge set. The Oracle RTA and Jelly
  adapters no longer drop edges through reachability-derived scoring filters or
  environment-variable bypasses.
- The retired TS token/object recognizer labels are gone from the call-algorithm
  vocabulary. Points-to remains the sole TS/JS indirect-call resolver.
- The unused sink-reachability data-flow edge kind is gone. Taint source-to-sink
  paths continue to come only from matched IFDS.

## Audit notes

- Remaining `recognizer` matches under `analysis/entrypoints` are the real
  framework-entrypoint extractors that create reachability roots, not an
  indirect-call resolver bank.
- Remaining `ts_object_model` matches under `analysis/semantic_graph` project
  allocation/property facts into the shared points-to constraints; they are
  inputs to the sole TS resolver, not a second resolver.
- Remaining `inflate` matches are Go RTA test/documentation wording about budget
  caps; no filter or alternate engine is attached to them.
- `policy_queries.rs` has no acceptance-search matches.
